Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

Last Updated: March 21, 2026

Information We Collect

Barangay Reserve collects the following information to provide our services:

  • Email Address: For authentication and account management
  • Name: For user identification and service personalization
  • Contact Number: For booking confirmations and notifications
  • Address: For service eligibility verification
  • Profile Photo: For user identification purposes
  • Verification Documents: For resident authentication

How We Use Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Authenticating users and verifying residency
  • Managing barangay facility reservations
  • Processing verification requests
  • Sending notifications and confirmations
  • Providing resident services
  • Improving system functionality

Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described in this policy.

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information

Google Authentication

We use Google Sign-In for authentication purposes. When you use Google Sign-In, we receive:

  • Login/Register Separation: Distinct flows for existing vs new users
  • Profile Configuration: Additional setup required for new users

This information is used only for account creation, authentication, and profile management.

Verification System Data

Our verification system collects and processes:

  • Verification Type: Unverified, Verified Non-Resident, or Verified Resident status
  • Discount Eligibility: 0%, 5%, or 10% discount rates based on verification
  • Verification Documents: Uploaded files for residency verification
  • Verification Status: Current state of verification process
  • Field Locking Data: Information about locked/unlocked profile fields

Booking and Ban System Data

For facility management, we collect:

  • Booking History: All past and current facility reservations
  • Rejection Reasons: Fake payment or insufficient payment flags
  • Strike Count: 3-strike ban system violation tracking
  • Ban Status: Current ban status and duration
  • Payment Records: Transaction history and payment verification

Auto-Fill and Synchronization

Our auto-fill system uses:

  • Profile Data: Personal information for form completion
  • Cross-Screen Sync: Data shared between booking forms and settings
  • Field Mapping: Automatic population of related fields
  • Validation Data: Information used for form validation

Enhanced User Rights

In addition to basic rights, you have the right to:

  • Verification Control: Manage verification status and document uploads
  • Auto-Fill Management: Control automatic data population settings
  • Profile Photo Control: Update or replace profile pictures
  • Booking History: Access complete booking records

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
